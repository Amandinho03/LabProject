Our instruments

The Open Science Lab has a suite of over 30 specialised, cutting-edge facilities, striving to keep at the frontiers of scientic discovery.

Gaining access to state-of-the-art laboratory equipment remains a difficult challenge for scientists in under-resourced regions. This is holding science back, creating a knowledge gap between the North and South, and resulting in brain drain.

Remote Access

Core Facilities

Open Science Lab is enabling scientists in under-resourced regions, to access sophisticated scientific equipment without leaving their home country. The scientists also receive training on how to use this equipment remotely.
